About

Each point is one column. Where the asset number is reasonably secure, the point sits at the position in Kent County Council's streetlight inventory; otherwise it sits where the phone's GPS placed the photo.

Confirmed: asset number painted on the column, read in full. Strong: partly legible, only one asset fits. Probable: partial or hand-painted number, or the column's place in a numbered run. Likely: nearest KCC column by GPS (tested at 93% right). Uncertain: several possibilities; the first listed is the best guess. No match: no KCC column within 45 m.

Variant codes: A1 Biggleston standard (CCC cypher on the door); A1-alt an altered A1; A2 fluted drum without cypher; B1/B2 DW Windsor reproductions; X-stub cut-down remains; X-mod modern replacement.

Asset numbers refer to KCC's inventory extract of 26 September 2026 and may be wrong. Number plates and faces in the photos have been blurred.
