About

Each point is one column. Where the asset number is reasonably secure, the point sits at the position in Kent County Council's streetlight inventory; otherwise it sits where the phone's GPS placed the photo.

Confirmed: asset number painted on the column, read in full. Strong: partly legible, only one asset fits. Probable: partial or hand-painted number, or the column's place in a numbered run. Likely: nearest KCC column by GPS (tested at 93% right). Uncertain: several possibilities; the first listed is the best guess. No match: no KCC column matched (the riverside path columns off Westwood Drive and Ambleside Place are City Council's, so not in KCC's inventory).

Variant codes: A1 Biggleston standard (CCC cypher on the door); A1-alt an altered A1; A2 fluted drum without cypher; A4 small-door pattern (short framed door, reeding all the way round the base); B1/B2 DW Windsor reproductions; X-stub cut-down remains; X-mod modern replacement.

Visual condition is an assessment of what can be seen in the survey photographs (2026); it is not a structural or electrical inspection. It covers overall visual condition, paintwork, external structure (e.g. a missing ladder-bar acorn counts as damaged), lantern, access door and colour. Parts not shown in any photo are marked "Not seen".

Asset numbers refer to KCC's inventory extract of 26 September 2026 and may be wrong. Number plates and faces in the photos have been blurred.
